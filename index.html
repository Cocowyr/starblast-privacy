Offline Games Privacy Policy

最后更新日期 / Last Updated: 2026-10-17

中文

感谢您使用「离线游戏」。这是一款离线可玩的三消闯关游戏。本政策说明我们在游戏进度存储与广告展示方面的隐私实践。

English

Thank you for using "Offline Games", an offline match-3 puzzle game. This policy explains our privacy practices regarding game progress storage and advertising.

1. 我们收集的信息 / Information We Collect

中文

  • 应用不要求注册、登录或提供任何个人身份信息。
  • 您的关卡进度、星星数、最高分、道具数量与设置偏好仅保存在本机设备（UserDefaults / 本地存储），不会上传任何服务器。
  • 应用无需联网即可完整游玩，游戏内容全部打包在应用内。

English

  • No registration, login, or personal identification is required.
  • Your level progress, star count, high scores, power-up inventory, and preferences are stored only on your device (UserDefaults / local storage) and are never uploaded to any server.
  • The game is fully playable offline; all game content is bundled inside the app.

2. 广告 / Advertising

中文

本应用接入穿山甲(Pangle)广告SDK，用于展示广告以维持免费服务。本应用内会展示由第三方广告平台提供的广告。

为完成广告的加载、展示频次控制、反作弊与效果归因，广告SDK可能会收集：设备标识(IDFA/OAID)、设备型号、操作系统版本、网络类型、粗略位置(非精确定位)等非个人设备信息。按照 Apple 要求，应用会通过「App 跟踪透明度（ATT）」弹窗征得知情同意：只有在您明确授权后，广告 SDK 才会访问广告标识符用于跨应用跟踪；若您拒绝，则仅使用不可关联到个人的基础设备信息用于广告投放，不会进行跨应用跟踪。您也可随时在系统「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」中关闭广告个性化。

本应用开发者自身不收集、不出售、亦不共享任何个人信息。广告相关的数据处理由上述第三方广告平台在其各自的隐私政策与 ATT 授权范围内独立完成，我们不会将您的账号、姓名、联系方式等个人身份信息提供给广告平台用于定向投放。您可随时在系统「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」中关闭广告跟踪。

English

This app integrates the Pangle ad SDK to display ads to keep the service free. It shows ads served by third-party advertising platforms.

To perform ad loading, frequency capping, anti-fraud, and attribution, the ad SDK may access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) along with non-personal device information such as device model, OS version, and network type. In accordance with Apple's requirements, the app requests consent through the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt: only after your explicit authorization will the ad SDK access the advertising identifier for cross-app tracking; if you decline, only non-personally-identifiable basic device information is used for ad delivery, and no cross-app tracking occurs.

The app developer itself does not collect, sell, or share any personal information. Ad-related data processing is performed independently by the third-party advertising platforms under their own privacy policies and within the scope of ATT authorization. We do not provide your account, name, contact details, or other personal identifiers to advertisers for targeted delivery. You can turn off ad tracking at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

3. 激励视频与游戏进度 / Rewarded Video & Progress

中文

应用中提供可选的激励视频广告：完整观看后可获得复活机会、解锁下一关卡或额外道具。是否观看完全由您自主选择，不观看不会影响游戏的正常通关流程 —— 所有关卡均可通过正常游玩逐关解锁。

English

The app offers optional rewarded video ads: watching one in full grants a revive, unlocks the next level, or awards extra power-ups. Watching is entirely your choice and never required to progress — every level can be unlocked through normal play.

English

We do not collect, sell, or share any personal information. Beyond the ad SDK calls explicitly disclosed above, your data does not leave your device.

5. 未成年人 / Children

English

This app does not collect any personal information from any user and does not attempt to identify a user's age. All game progress and settings are stored only on your device. This app is suitable for users aged 12 and above.
